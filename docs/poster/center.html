2Safe route

Plan paths that avoid cells predicted to ignite before the dog gets through.

3Escape path

Always keep a way out that the forecast says will stay clear.

4Scout

Check the fire edge and the planned containment line before crews commit.

5Feedback

Send back real fire position, wind and fuel so the next forecast is better.
