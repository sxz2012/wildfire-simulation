2:45 PM Launch

Steep, brushy slopes, burning since midnight. Too dangerous for a crew, so the dog leaves staging.

2

2:45 PM Plan

The blue route keeps 100 m from the forecast fire, with 30 min in hand, slower in brush and uphill.

3

3:15 PM Wind shift

The real fire turns; the forecast doesn't know, so the prediction needs a rerun. The green escape path stays open.

4

4:45 PM Scout

It reaches its scout spot, as close as the 100 m rule allows.

5

4:45 PM Measure

Its sensors find wind from the northwest, not the west, and 40 °C, not 35. The prediction reruns: the fire is coming, so the dog re-plans and heads home.

6

~7:15 PM Home safe

Back before the fire arrives. The crew plans with a better forecast.
