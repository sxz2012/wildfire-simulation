Fire-aware robot dogs: machine-learning wildfire forecasts for safe navigation and scouting

Shawn Zeng
Monta Vista High School, Cupertino, California

Legged robots could scout a wildfire edge in place of firefighters, but only if they know where the fire will be. We combine a machine-learning spread forecast with a terrain-aware route planner that keeps a robot dog ahead of the predicted fire and holds an escape path home. In a simulated mission on real Big Sur terrain, an unforecast wind shift cut forecast agreement from 82% to 61%; the dog's field readings restored it to 80%, and the dog returned safely.
