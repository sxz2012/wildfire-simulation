The fire has burned since midnight on steep, brushy slopes. Too dangerous to send a crew to look, so the dog leaves staging.
23:00 PM · Plan
On its forecast, the route (blue) passes every spot 30+ min before the fire, slower through brush and uphill.
33:30 PM · Wind shift
The real fire turns. The forecast doesn't know: match 82% → 61%. The escape path (green) stays open.
43:45 PM · Measure
Its sensors see the real fire coming: 40 °C, wind from 45°, not the forecast's 35 °C from 270°. It sends the readings back: match 61% → 80%.
54:45 PM · Scout
On the corrected forecast it re-plans, reaches the fire edge and scouts it.
6~8 PM · Home safe
Back at staging before the fire arrives. The crew plans with a better forecast.
