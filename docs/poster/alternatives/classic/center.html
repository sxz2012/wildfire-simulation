Fire-Aware Robot Dogs

Machine-Learning Wildfire Forecasts for Safe Navigation and Scouting
Shawn Zeng · Monta Vista High School
Ongoing work · currently in simulation
built & tested in simulation
robot dog

1Predict

The model forecasts when each 50 m cell will catch fire. Designed to run on the dog or its controller.

In: satellite fire map, weather, terrain, land cover. Out: ignition time for every cell, in 15‑min steps.

2Safe route

Plan paths that avoid cells predicted to ignite before the dog gets through.

Fastest-path search: 1.5 m/s on open ground, 0.7× in forest, 0.5× in brush; slower uphill than downhill; no slopes over 35°. Every cell is crossed at least 30 min before the forecast says it ignites; the route is re-checked every step.

3Escape path

Always keep a way out that the forecast says will stay clear.

A path home that aims for a stricter 60 min margin (30 min if it must), re-planned from wherever the dog is. If the way forward closes, the dog retreats along it.

4Scout

Walk to the fire edge, measure the real conditions, and get home before the fire arrives.

Holds 30 min at the edge (simulated sensors), and leaves early if the fire gets within 30 min of it.

5Feedback

Send back measured wind, temperature, humidity and the real fire edge so the next forecast is better.

The forecast is re-run from the measured conditions and the true fire position; the dog re-plans on it. Demo: match 61% → 80%.
