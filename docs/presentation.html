Environmental Impact Estimation

Emissions calculated per hotspot detection using published emission factors (van der Werf et al., Global Fire Emissions Database).

CO₂ ~15 tonnes per hectare burned
PM2.5 ~0.4 tonnes per hectare burned
CO ~1.2 tonnes per hectare burned

Methodology

Each VIIRS satellite detection covers approximately 14 hectares (375m × 375m pixel). Emissions are estimated by multiplying detected area by fuel-type-specific emission factors. Real-time estimates update as the timeline scrubs through the fire progression. The "Car Years Equivalent" metric converts CO₂ to the number of average passenger vehicles driven for one year (4.6 tonnes CO₂/car/year, EPA).

All data sources are free and publicly accessible
